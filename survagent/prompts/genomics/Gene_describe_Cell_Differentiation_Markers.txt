## Background Knowledge
- **Significance of the Cell Differentiation Markers Gene Group**: Cell differentiation genes primarily regulate the process by which cells transition from an undifferentiated or poorly differentiated state to acquiring specific morphological and functional characteristics. These genes orchestrate the formation of distinctive features in various tissues and organs by modulating the gene expression profiles within cells.During tumor development, dysregulated expression of cell differentiation genes often leads tumor cells to exhibit an undifferentiated or poorly differentiated state. This state is strongly correlated with high malignancy grade and poor prognosis in cancer.
